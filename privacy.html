Slotly 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新 Last updated: 2026-10-23

我們收集什麼 What we collect

  • 主人帳號：Email、顯示名稱、時區、可預約時間、預約類型。
    Host accounts: email, display name, timezone, availability and event types.
  • 預約：預約人的名字、Email、留言、時間。只有該主人看得到。
    Bookings: invitee name, email, note and time — visible only to that host.
  • 主人選擇連結 Google 帳號時：Google 帳號 Email，以及一組授權碼（refresh token）。
    If a host connects Google: their Google account email and an OAuth refresh token.

Google 資料怎麼使用 How Google data is used

Slotly 只用 calendar.events 權限做一件事：有人預約時，在主人自己的 Google 日曆建立一個附 Google Meet 連結的活動；預約取消時刪除這個活動。不讀取主人的其他日曆內容，不分享、不出售任何 Google 資料。
Slotly uses the calendar.events scope only to create an event with a Google Meet link on the host's own calendar when someone books, and to delete it when the booking is cancelled. It does not read other calendar data, and never shares or sells Google user data. Slotly's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

儲存與刪除 Storage and deletion

資料存在 Supabase（Postgres）。主人可以隨時在「個人設定」取消連結 Google，授權碼會立即刪除；也可以到 Google 帳戶權限 撤銷。要刪除帳號和全部資料，請來信。
Data is stored in Supabase (Postgres). Hosts can disconnect Google at any time in Profile, which deletes the token immediately, or revoke access in their Google Account. To delete your account and all data, email us.
